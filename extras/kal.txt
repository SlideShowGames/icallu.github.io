For all media licensed under the KAL or
KAL2 license, the content must only be
shared with the people intended to at
the start, it cannot be shared or leaked
until exactly 2 years after the creation
date has passed, and then it is public
domain.

Under KAL2 all media or content licensed
under KAL2 is ONLY the legal property of
the creator, legal action will be taken
on those who claim ownership over
content under KAL2 as a local entity no
matter if its public on the internet or
not.
KAL3 Adds the following extension;
The item that was declared to be licensed under KAL, shall be under the ownership of the by BLOOD PARENT of the actually creator until the 2 years has passed. An item cannot be under the ownership of a legal guardian. In the case of O.P. Logs, they are the 1st Amendment, and follow free speech, and free press.
